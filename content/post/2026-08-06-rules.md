My reign as world king will be firm, but fair. The following laws supersede all other legislation.
There is no room for compromise under my benevolent dictatorship.
This is an evolving list. As an autocrat I reserve the right to change or extend the rules at any time.
All other legislation and enforcement is devolved to the respective existing regional authorities.

## Fake grass means real prison

Installation of astro-turf, i.e. fake grass, outdoors is an immediate custodial sentence.
Some prison overcrowding is to be expected in the transition phase.

## Dubbed films are banned

Dubbing in films and TV series is awful and nothing will convince me otherwise.
Apologists may claim 'actually there are some really good dubbing actors'. No, wrong.
Turn on subtitles or shoot a new adaptation. It may even grow the local film industry.

*Exceptions:* children's programming and cartoons, which are dubbed anyway.

## Only one bike rack allowed

The problem of designing the perfect universal cycle rack was solved long ago; the answer is the Sheffield stand.
All public and commercial bike racks must therefore be Sheffield stands.
Where cycle parking is provided, it must meet this minimum standard.

## Crew cabs to be crushed

You may have a normal pickup truck or a four-door vehicle. You may not have both.

## No such thing as a cycling rucksack

This law has several parts, each of which relating to marketing of practical cycling equipment.

1. It is forbidden to market a rucksack as suitable for cycling without prominently mentioning the obviously superior alternatives first: panniers, front bags and trailers. Downsides of cycling with a backpack must be highlighted.
2. It is forbidden to market any bicycle as suitable for commuting if it does not have mounting eyelets for full-length mudguards (fenders) and a pannier rack. Mudguards and a rack do not, however, have to be pre-installed or sold with the bike.
3. The term "gravel bike" is generally banned.

------

That is all. Everything else can carry on as normal.
